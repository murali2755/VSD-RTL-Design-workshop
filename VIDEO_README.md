## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. RTL Design Workshop — Day 1 to Day 5

- RTL design, simulation, and synthesis
- Flip-flops and synchronous/asynchronous resets
- PVT corners, Liberty files, and hierarchy
- Combinational and sequential logic optimisation
- Gate-Level Simulation (GLS)
- Blocking and non-blocking assignments
- Latches, case statements, and generate loops

### 2. Physical Design — Day 6 to Day 10

- OpenLane RTL-to-GDSII flow
- Synthesis and standard-cell analysis
- Floorplanning and power planning
- Placement and clock tree synthesis (CTS)
- CMOS inverter, SPICE, and layout
- Setup and hold timing analysis
- Global and detailed routing
- DRC, LVS, antenna checks, and sign-off concepts

### 3. VSDBabySoC Project

- RVMYTH RISC-V processor core
- PLL and DAC integration
- RTL simulation and synthesis
- Gate-level netlist and simulation
- Floorplanning and macro placement
- Clock tree synthesis and routing
- Post-route STA across 16 PVT corners
- Critical-path and timing-report analysis

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- Verilog HDL
- Icarus Verilog
- GTKWave
- Yosys
- OpenLane
- OpenROAD
- OpenSTA
- Magic
- SKY130HD PDK

## 🎯 Key Learnings

This workshop helped me understand the complete RTL-to-GDSII design flow, from RTL coding and functional verification to synthesis, physical implementation, and timing analysis.

Working on VSDBabySoC helped me connect theoretical VLSI concepts with practical implementation and analysis.
